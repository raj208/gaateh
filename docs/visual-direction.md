# Tribal Match Visual Direction

## Tone

- Investor-serious, not theatrical
- Premium modern startup, not trend-chasing
- Warm, calm, and trust-led rather than romantic or entertainment-focused
- Culturally grounded without leaning on stereotypes or decorative shorthand

## Color Usage

- Use warm neutrals as the dominant surface language
- Reserve the earthy accent for trust cues, highlights, and focused calls to action
- Prefer restrained contrast, soft borders, and layered whites over loud gradients
- Keep each section readable first; color should support hierarchy, not become the message

## Typography

- Serif display type is for conviction and gravity in major headings
- Sans type is for interface clarity, body copy, and operational trust
- Headings should feel measured and high-trust, never flashy or ad-like
- Body copy should keep line lengths disciplined and readable on mobile

## Motion Philosophy

- Motion should feel expensive because it is restrained
- Use soft fades, slight upward movement, and small stagger timing
- Prioritize spatial clarity over spectacle
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and avoid motion that blocks comprehension

## Asset Style

- Prefer abstract SVG systems, line grids, soft orbs, signal cards, and minimal iconography
- Decorative assets should suggest infrastructure, credibility, and calm depth
- When future imagery is added, it should feel documentary or product-real, not stock-marketing polished

## Approved Future Visuals

- Real logo and refined wordmark system
- Real product interface captures once the UI is stable
- Trust architecture diagrams that explain the system honestly
- Optional founder portrait if it adds credibility and is shot cleanly
- Minimal brand illustration system based on geometry and trust signals

## Avoid

- Loud gradients, neon accents, or app-store style hype visuals
- Swipe-app tropes, hearts, chat bubbles, or flirt-oriented iconography
- Stock handshake imagery or generic startup-office photography
- Any culturally stereotyped or tokenizing tribal imagery
- Fake dashboards that imply product depth that does not exist yet
