# Future Asset Recommendations

## Recommended To Collect Later

- Real logo and final wordmark lockup
- One or two real product UI screenshots once the product surface is stable
- A clean trust architecture diagram explaining verification, privacy, and moderation layers
- Optional founder portrait with neutral, professional art direction
- Optional workflow graphic showing introduction flow from onboarding to trusted discovery
- Optional verification flow visual that explains how trust signals appear without oversharing
- Optional subtle India-first map treatment only if it remains abstract and restrained
- Optional minimal illustration set for trust, privacy, and serious introductions

## Recommended Usage Notes

- Product visuals should be honest and clearly represent what exists
- Diagrams should explain system logic, not decorate empty space
- Portraits, if used, should feel credible and modern rather than staged like recruitment stock
- Any India-first visual should stay abstract, respectful, and non-stereotyped

## Strong Avoid List

- No cliché wedding or dating imagery
- No generic handshake stock photos
- No culturally stereotyped tribal imagery
- No visuals that feel exploitative, tokenizing, or anthropological
- No overly romanticized app-store style marketing
- No fake dashboard screenshots
- No random illustration packs that break the current visual restraint
- No decorative visuals that imply a consumer entertainment brand
